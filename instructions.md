DOCUMENT STATUS
======================================================================

This document describes an experimental manufacturing process.

The resulting material should be described only as:

"Experimental hemp seed protein powder paper roll, untested."

The process does not demonstrate that the product is:

- office paper
- copy paper
- printer paper
- drawing paper
- archival paper
- packaging paper
- recyclable paper
- compostable paper
- biodegradable paper

unless independently tested and validated.
